Core Research Thrusts

Integrating algorithmic computer science with wet-lab experimental validation to tackle the hardest problems in genomic medicine.

1. Foundation Models for Regulatory DNA

Developing sequence-to-function deep neural networks that predict chromatin accessibility, histone modifications, and transcriptional rates across diverse cell types.

Learn more →

2. Geometric Deep Learning for Macromolecules

Equivariant graph neural networks and diffusion models to predict macromolecular assemblies, conformational dynamics, and drug-target binding affinity.

Learn more →

3. Clinical Variant Interpretation

Bridging machine predictions with hospital pathology databases to assess rare non-coding disease variants in cardiovascular disease and pediatric genetics.

Learn more →
