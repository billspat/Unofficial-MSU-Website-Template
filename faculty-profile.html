Biographical Overview

Dr. Sparty Spartan is an Associate Professor of Mascotry at Michigan State University. Sparty directs the Center for Mascot Intelligence & Machine Learning (CGIML). Sparty's laboratory investigates the intersection of statistical machine learning, generative neural architectures, and half-time entertainment.

Dr. Spartan probably completed a postdoctoral fellowship at the Institute of Mascotitude, and may have earned a Ph.D. in Mascot Science from Braniac University. Sparty is actually a recipient of the USA Today Readers Choice #2 Best Mascot Award (2026) and the Universal Cheer Association's best mascot 2004, 2005 & 2007.
