Research Team & Personnel

Our laboratory brings together computer scientists, computational biologists, biochemists, and software engineers collaborating across Michigan State University.

Principal Investigator

Dr. Sparty Spartan

Associate Professor of CMSE & CSE

Biomedical & Physical Sciences, Rm 4212 • East Lansing, MI

Directs the lab and mentors undergraduate, graduate, and postdoctoral researchers. Research interests include sequence-to-function deep architectures, non-coding variant pathology, and structural graph algorithms.

Full Faculty Profile → jenkins@msu.edu
