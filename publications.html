2026 Nature Biotechnology Article

Geometric Graph Transformers for Deciphering Enhancer-Promoter Interactivity

Jenkins, S., Zhao, Y., Brooks, D., & Miller, R.

Nature Biotechnology 44, 301–315 (2026). DOI: 10.1038/s41587-026-00123-x

View Abstract & Key Findings
Non-coding mutations account for over 90% of disease-associated variants identified by genome-wide association studies, yet their functional targets remain ambiguous. We introduce GeoLoop, an SE(3)-equivariant graph transformer that maps three-dimensional chromatin folds and spatial contact loops directly from mammalian DNA sequence, outperforming previous convolutional baselines by 28% in identifying causal regulatory hubs in cardiac cell lineages.

2025 Cell Genomics Open Access

Sequence-to-Expression Modeling Identifies Pathogenic Non-Coding Variants in Congenital Heart Disease

Kaufman, L., Patel, A., Sharma, P., & Jenkins, S.

Cell Genomics 5(8), 100452 (2025). DOI: 10.1016/j.xgen.2025.100452

View Abstract & Key Findings
We present a targeted saturation mutagenesis screen evaluated against deep sequence-to-expression models in human embryonic stem cell-derived cardiomyocytes. The architecture pinpointed 14 de novo non-coding mutations disrupting cardiac transcription factor NKX2-5 binding, validated via CRISPR-Cas9 base editing.
DOI: 10.1016/j.xgen.2025.100452 PDF (2.4 MB)
2025 NeurIPS 2025 Spotlight

Diffusion Models on Riemannian Manifolds for Macromolecular Assembly Conformations

Sharma, P., Zhao, Y., & Jenkins, S.

Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS) 38, 14220–14234 (2025).

View Abstract & Key Findings
Simulating large macromolecular transitions requires capturing continuous spatial rotations and translations. We develop a score-based generative model defined directly over SO(3) x R3 manifolds, achieving accurate thermal fluctuations in protein-protein complexes without costly all-atom molecular dynamics.
