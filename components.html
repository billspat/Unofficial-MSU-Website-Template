2. Official MSU Wordmark Lockups (Adaptive Color)

The official Michigan State University wordmark and Spartan helmet are vector-rendered with fill="currentColor". This allows the lockup to automatically adapt: pure white on dark headers/footers, and official Spartan Green (#18453B) on light backgrounds.

On Dark Background (bg-msu-green / #18453B)
class="fill-current text-white"
On Light Background (bg-white / #FFFFFF)
class="fill-current text-msu-green"
Inline SVG (Recommended) Directly inherits parent font color (text-white or text-msu-green) with zero flash of unstyled content.
Static Asset (Green) dist/img/msu-wordmark-green.svg for standalone <img> tags on white pages.
Static Asset (White) dist/img/msu-wordmark-white.svg for standalone <img> tags on dark backgrounds.
