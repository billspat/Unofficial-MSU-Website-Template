1. Enviroweather

The Enviroweather Service develops and delivers a sustainable weather-based information system for the state of Michigan. Enviroweather operates and collects readings from nearly 100 industrial-grade weather stations in Michigan that feed models that helps users make pest, plant production, and natural resource management decisions in Michigan.
